# 🚩 Welcome to the ctf-toolkit

**A curated arsenal of tools, commands, and workflows for Capture The Flag competitions.**

This toolkit is designed to be your quick-reference field guide during CTFs. It cuts through the noise and provides the exact commands and syntax you need to solve challenges in Recon, Crypto, Web, and Pwn.
